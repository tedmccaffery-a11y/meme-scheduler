Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Summary

Meme Scheduler is a personal tool used only by its owner. It does not collect, sell or share anyone's personal data.

2. What the app stores

When the owner connects their own TikTok account, TikTok issues access tokens. The app stores these tokens only on the owner's own computer, so it can publish videos to that account. The app also reads the account's basic information (display name, username and allowed privacy settings), as TikTok requires before posting. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's.

3. Other people

The app has no other users and does not access other TikTok users' data, followers, messages or analytics.

4. Third parties

Videos are published through TikTok's official Content Posting API, and TikTok's Privacy Policy applies to them. Stock photos may be downloaded from Pixabay, but no personal data is sent to Pixabay.

5. Deleting data

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok's settings (Security → Manage app permissions) and delete the stored tokens from their computer.

6. Contact

For questions, open an issue on the GitHub repository that hosts this page.
